# Factions Overview

Four playable factions compete over a world overgrown by the Vines. Beyond them, a
fifth force occupies the world itself: Neutral wild zones, feral blooms, mutated life,
and dormant defenses that exist primarily as PvE pressure rather than a standard empire.

Shared canon reference: [../LORE.md](../LORE.md).

## Shared Conflict

All factions are reacting to the same broken system:

- The Amuns created the Vine network and want to reclaim or recalibrate it.
- The Radix emerged from the broken network and wants the right to exist.
- The Legion believes controlled Vine ascension is humanity's future.
- The Remnants reject forced transformation and fight to preserve human autonomy.
- The Wild is the uncontrolled fallout that makes PvE maps dangerous for everyone.

The Current War takes place in the late 2080s, after the 2069 Awakening and nearly two decades
of faction formation. The Legion and Remnants are mature ideologies, the Radix is coherent enough
to act deliberately, and the Amuns return to a project that has been drifting outside their control
for years.

| Faction | Type | Playstyle |
|---|---|---|
| [The Amuns](Amuns/The_Amuns.md) | Alien architects | Elite, advanced tech, dimensional anchors |
| [The Radix](Radix/The_Radix.md) | Sentient vines | Creep-based, bio-economy, no power grid |
| [The Legion](The_Legion/The_Legion.md) | Vine-embracing human theocracy | Hybrid infantry, doctrine, ritual infrastructure |
| [The Remnants](Remnants/The_Remnants.md) | Human survivors | Salvage, burn-clearing, local autonomy |

## Where to find information

Each faction folder contains a single canonical overview file with:
- **Lore / Origins** — backstory and motivation
- **Economy** — how they harvest Vine resources
- **Tech Tree** — Tier 1 build order
- **Buildings** — costs and stats
- **Units** — stats and abilities

Buildings, units, and current tech notes live inside those faction files rather than in
separate cross-cutting folders.
