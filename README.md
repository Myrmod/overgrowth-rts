# Overgrowth RTS

<img alt="image" src="assets\logos\overgrowth_logo.png" />

Overgrowth is an RTS about a broken Earth where an ancient alien bioforming system has awakened without its makers. Four playable factions fight over the same Vine-choked planet.

## Lore Summary

Long before recorded history, The Amuns seeded Earth with the Vines: a dormant
bio-terraforming and energy-harvesting network tied to hidden anchor sites. After the Ash War shattered human civilization, desperate researchers discovered one of those
sites in 2069 and over-amplified it while searching for a new energy source.

The Awakening gave humanity power, but it also broke the Amun limiters. The Vines spread globally, adapted to violence, and developed a coherent intelligence known as the Radix. Through the 2070s and early 2080s, humanity's split hardened into the Remnants, who see the Vines as invasion and forced mutation, and the Legion, who treat controlled Vine ascension as the only path
to survival. By the late 2080s, the Current War begins as the Amuns return to reclaim and recalibrate what they consider their damaged design.

## Installing Proprietary Assets

This repository contains placeholder assets.
If you want to use the real assets, then you have to ask. We needed to do that due to licencing issues.

The assets need to be placed under `assets_override`. The path is defined in the requiring .tscn files.
